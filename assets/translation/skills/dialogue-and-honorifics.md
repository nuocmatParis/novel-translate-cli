Honorifics follow the actual Japanese source and scoped series conventions.
Never omit -san/-sama/-kun/-chan/-tan because of intimacy, RTAS or an emotional score.
If the source switches to a bare name, reflect the switch. Do not insert a name
or suffix where the source does not name the listener. Speaker → listener is
directional; do not apply the reverse pair automatically. Distinguish narration,
thoughts and spoken dialogue. Pronouns depend on the present scene, uncertainty
and known relationships at its story position. Do not import future identities.
Use supplied known_entities IDs for confirmed aliases. Unknown actors need
stable anonymous IDs supported by this source; never merge them into a secret
identity without explicit source evidence. Narration does not invent a listener.
